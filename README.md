## Who qualifies for the Google AI Pro student discount?

The U.S. student offer is not available to every person with a school-related email address. Google’s eligibility requirements include several conditions:

1. You must be an eligible college or higher-education student in the United States.
2. You must be at least 18 years old.
3. You must verify your student status through SheerID.
4. You need a personal Google Account.
5. You must add a valid form of payment during signup.
6. The offer must be available for your account and region at the time you apply.

Google says both new users and students whose 2025 AI Pro trial has expired may qualify for the current offer. Student status must be verified each year to maintain access.

A school-issued Google Workspace account may not work for this offer. Google’s terms state that student offers are for individual use and are not available on school-issued accounts. Use a personal Google Account during the enrollment process unless Google specifically tells you otherwise.

Family-plan membership can also affect eligibility. Google says family-group members may need to leave the shared plan or have the plan manager cancel the current membership before enrolling in a new student offer. Memberships obtained through certain third-party partners, Google Fi, or similar arrangements may also be excluded.

## How to claim Google AI Pro for free as a student

The process is fairly short, but the important part is using the correct Google account and completing verification before assuming the offer has been activated.

### 1. Open Google’s student offer page

Start from Google’s Gemini student page or Google One’s student offer flow. Google’s support instructions direct eligible students to the student offer page, where they can select the available Google AI plan for their region.

Avoid starting from a general Google One pricing page if your goal is specifically the student promotion. The normal pricing page may show paid plans without immediately displaying the student trial.

### 2. Sign in with a personal Google Account

Use the personal Google Account that you actually intend to keep using with Gemini, Gmail, Drive, and Google Photos.

This matters because the offer is tied to the account that completes verification. Switching accounts later can create duplicate subscriptions or leave the student plan attached to the wrong profile.

### 3. Complete SheerID verification

Google uses SheerID to verify student status. You may be asked to provide a school email address or upload documentation if automatic verification does not work.

Possible verification documents can vary by country and school. If your school email is not accepted immediately, follow the verification instructions rather than repeatedly submitting random documents. A failed automatic check does not necessarily mean you are ineligible.

### 4. Add a payment method

Google requires a valid form of payment during signup even though the first twelve months are free. This is a standard trial requirement, but it is also the detail most likely to surprise people later.

The subscription is scheduled to renew at the regular price after the free period. Google currently lists that price as $19.99 per month for the U.S. Google AI Pro plan.

### 5. Confirm the renewal date

After enrollment, check the subscription details in your Google account. Record the date when the twelve-month offer ends and confirm the post-trial price shown for your account.

If you do not want to continue, cancel before the free period ends. Google says you can cancel at any time, but the exact effect of cancellation can depend on the account and offer terms.

## Which plan makes sense after the free year?

The answer depends on how you use Gemini and Google storage.

### Google AI Plus may be enough for lighter use

AI Plus is the lower-priced option for people who want additional Gemini access and more storage without paying for the full Pro tier. It may suit students who mainly use Gemini for short explanations, writing help, occasional image generation, or basic organization.

The tradeoff is lower usage access and fewer advanced benefits than Google AI Pro. If you frequently upload large documents, work with long research tasks, or use advanced models and creative tools, the lower tier may become restrictive.

### Google AI Pro is the practical student upgrade

Google AI Pro is the closest match for students who use Gemini regularly. The plan includes 5 TB of storage and expanded access to Google’s AI tools, along with integrations into Google’s productivity products.

The regular annual price lowers the effective monthly cost compared with paying month to month, but the student promotion is clearly better during the free period if you qualify. Before allowing the paid renewal, compare your actual usage with the $19.99 monthly price.

### Google AI Ultra is aimed at heavy users

Ultra provides higher limits, more storage, and additional benefits such as YouTube Premium individual in supported markets. Google lists it at $99.99 per month, which puts it in a different category from a typical student subscription.

For most students, Ultra is difficult to justify unless they use advanced AI tools heavily for development, research, video generation, or other demanding workflows. Paying for the highest tier simply because it exists is an expensive way to discover that your normal assignments only needed Pro.
